Our Roots

A STORY TOLD
IN EVERY BITE

It started in a small backyard in Imus, with a rusty grill, big dreams, and one stubborn question: why does everyone settle for average burgers and basic wings?

Our founder, a self-taught cook with a passion for bold flavors, spent years perfecting the smash burger technique and testing wing sauces on anyone who would come over for dinner. Friends, family, neighbors — they all kept coming back for more. That's when we knew we were onto something.

In 2019, Pakpak was born — named after the Filipino word for "wings," and a nod to the clapping sound of a crowd showing love. From a humble food stall, we've grown into a full-fledged restaurant while keeping the same fire, the same recipes, and the same conviction: every guest deserves a meal worth remembering.

We're not a franchise. We're not trying to be the biggest. We're trying to be the best. And every day, we earn that title one plate at a time.

2017
THE BACKYARD EXPERIMENTS

Our founder starts testing burger and wing recipes from home, hosting weekend cookouts that become the stuff of local legend.

2019
PAKPAK IS BORN

A food stall opens in Imus, Cavite. On opening day, we run out of everything within three hours. We knew we were ready.

2021
THE PERMANENT HOME

With a loyal following built entirely by word-of-mouth, Pakpak moves into its first permanent restaurant space, growing the menu and the team.

TODAY
STILL HUNGRY. STILL GROWING.

Same recipes, same heart, bigger kitchen. We're still the same crew chasing the perfect bite — and we won't stop until we get there.
